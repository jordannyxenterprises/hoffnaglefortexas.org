Where I stand

50/50 Custody

Equal parenting as the default in Texas family courts. Children deserve both their mother and father — from day one.

Eliminate Property Taxes

End property taxes on Texas homes. Working families should own their houses outright, not rent them from the government.

Trade & Apprenticeship Schools

Real trade and apprenticeship programs in every Texas high school — paths to good careers without crippling student debt.

Honest School Lunches

A full investigation into what our kids are actually being served. Real food on real plates — not bureaucratic checkboxes.

I-35E ↔ I-45 Bypass

Build the long-overdue bypass south of the Trinity. Connect southern Dallas, relieve gridlock, deliver infrastructure that actually gets built.

Term Limits

Term limits for every political office in Texas. Career politicians forget who they work for — power belongs with the people.
