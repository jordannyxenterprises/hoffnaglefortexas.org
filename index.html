Where I stand

50/50 Custody

Equal custody as the default starting standard for every fit parent. Real enforcement when orders are violated, children’s sports and activities written in as a required consideration, and no child support obligation for parents earning $50,000 a year or less.

Eliminate Property Taxes

End property taxes for Texas homeowners. You paid for your home. It should be yours — not rented back from the government every year.

Trade & Work-Study Credit

Require high schools to accept work-study for juniors and seniors. Students earn credit for 10 or more hours a week in a licensed trade, construction, networking, data cabling, or computer science.

School Choice for Homeschoolers

Homeschooling parents pay the same taxes as everyone else. Make school vouchers available to them, too.

Honest School Lunches

A full investigation into what our schools are actually serving our kids. Real meals on the tray — not the cheapest thing that clears a federal checkbox.

Highways, Not Tollways

Build the I-35E to I-45 bypass south of the Trinity and add real highway capacity between southern Dallas and downtown. Free to drive. No tolls.

Term Limits

Term limits for every political office in Texas. Career politicians forget who they work for — power belongs with the people.
