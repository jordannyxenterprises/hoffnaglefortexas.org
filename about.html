Meet Jordan Hoffnagle

I’m Jordan Hoffnagle, a father and Texan running for Texas House District 100 because the families of southern Dallas deserve a representative who actually fights for them — not party bosses, not lobbyists, not special interests in Austin.

For too long, working families in our district have been ignored, overtaxed, and forced to navigate broken systems that put politics over people. Career politicians have forgotten who they work for. I’m running to change that.

I believe parents — both parents — should lead their children’s lives, and that Texas family courts should treat fit mothers and fathers as equals from day one. I believe Texans should own their homes, not rent them from the government through endless property taxes. I believe our kids deserve real education and real food — not bureaucratic checkboxes. And I believe District 100 deserves infrastructure that actually gets built, starting with the long-overdue I-35E to I-45 bypass south of the Trinity.

This campaign isn’t about politics as usual. It’s about delivering results for the families who have been waiting too long for someone to show up.

Priorities

50/50 Custody

Texas family courts should treat both fit parents as equals from the start. Children deserve both their mother and father — and the court-appointed actors making life-altering decisions for our kids must be held accountable.

Eliminate Property Taxes

End property taxes on Texas homes. Working families should own their homes outright — not rent them from the government in perpetuity.

Trade & Apprenticeship Schools

Real trade and apprenticeship programs in every Texas high school. Our kids deserve paths to good careers that don’t require crippling student debt.

Honest School Lunches

A full investigation into what our schools are actually feeding our children. Real food, real nutrition, real transparency — nothing less.

I-35E ↔ I-45 Bypass

Build the long-overdue bypass south of the Trinity. Southern Dallas has waited decades for the infrastructure that connects our communities and relieves the worst gridlock in the region.

Term Limits

Term limits for every political office in Texas. Career politicians forget who they work for. Power should rotate through the people — not pool in Austin.
