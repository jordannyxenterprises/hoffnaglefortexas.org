Meet Jordan Hoffnagle

I’m Jordan Hoffnagle, a father and a Texan running for Texas House District 100 because the families of southern Dallas deserve a representative who fights for them — not party bosses, not lobbyists, not special interests in Austin.

Working families in this district have been ignored, overtaxed, and forced to navigate systems that put politics ahead of people. Career politicians have forgotten who they work for. I’m running to change that.

My first fight is our family courts. Children need both parents, and Texas should start every custody case at 50/50 between two fit parents — then enforce the orders it hands down. A parent who follows the rules should not lose years with their kids because the other side knows the system has no teeth.

Beyond the courthouse, the priorities are just as plain. Texans should own their homes outright, not rent them back from the government through property taxes. Our high schools should hand juniors and seniors a paycheck and a trade, not just a brochure for a university they’ll spend twenty years paying off. Our kids should get real food at lunch. And southern Dallas should get the highway capacity it has been promised for decades — built, finished, and free to drive.

This campaign isn’t about politics as usual. It’s about delivering for families who have waited far too long for someone to show up.

Priorities

50/50 Custody

Equal custody as the default starting standard for every fit parent. Enforcement with real consequences for repeat violations, children’s sports and extracurriculars written into standard orders as a required consideration, and no child support obligation for parents earning $50,000 a year or less.

Eliminate Property Taxes

End property taxes for Texas homeowners. You already paid for your home — you shouldn’t have to keep paying the government for the right to keep it.

Trade & Work-Study Credit

Require high schools to accept work-study programs for juniors and seniors, granting credit for 10 or more hours a week in a licensed trade, construction, networking, data cabling, or computer science. A diploma and a career, not a diploma and a loan.

School Choice for Homeschoolers

Homeschooling parents pay the same school taxes as every other family in Texas. School vouchers should be available to them as well.

Honest School Lunches

A full investigation into what our schools are actually feeding our children — what’s served, what it costs, and who profits. Real food, real nutrition, real transparency.

Highways, Not Tollways

Build the I-35E to I-45 bypass south of the Trinity and add highway capacity for the commute between southern Dallas and downtown. Paid for once, free to drive — no tolls.

Term Limits

Term limits for every political office in Texas. Career politicians forget who they work for. Power should rotate through the people — not pool in Austin.
